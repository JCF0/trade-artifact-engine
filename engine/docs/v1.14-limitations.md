# Artifact v1.14 Limitations

Artifact v1.14 Wallet-Wide Bounded Acquisition v1 is deliberately conservative:

- Completeness is provider-attested, not a trustless cryptographic proof against malicious or jointly inconsistent providers.
- The current adapter supports Helius plus Solana RPC only.
- Acquisition supports Solana mainnet-beta only and pins its genesis hash.
- Only `lookback_7d_v1`, `lookback_30d_v1`, `lookback_90d_v1`, and `lookback_180d_v1` are understood.
- There is no arbitrary historical end date and pagination must begin at latest state.
- High-activity wallets may exhaust safe page, transaction, retry, or timeout budgets; the result fails rather than truncates.
- One source transaction may produce at most one supported event.
- Multi-operation, multi-swap, swap-plus-distinct-transfer, batch, and multiple-output transactions are unsupported or ambiguous rather than partially accepted.
- Fee-payer and wallet-ownership policy is conservative. Delegated or sponsored swaps are not supported events.
- v1.14 has no live marks. Open and realized-partial unrealized PnL therefore remains unavailable.
- Accounting remains raw quote only. There is no cross-quote or USD normalization.
- No exact retained finalized RPC transcript exists; exact RPC transcript retention remains future evidence work.
- The tracked tree intentionally contains exactly five retained Helius fixture bodies used for deterministic replay: `jup_buy`, `jup_sell`, `ray_buy`, `ray_sell`, and `jupiter_close_account_swap`. Controlled-live raw responses are not retained.
- Finalized RPC pages are synthetic fixtures. Orca, UNKNOWN, failed, mixed-disposition, ambiguity, and provider-error bodies are also synthetic unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- There is no hosted persistence, authentication, worker system, product API, or product UI.
- There is no publication, package-store commit, upload, signing, minting, or deployment in this boundary.
- There is no privacy guarantee against correlation with public-chain wallet activity.
- Authoritative construction boundaries enforce native Solana identity grammar and byte width: 64-byte Base58 transaction signatures and `tx_hash` values, and 32-byte Base58 wallets, mints, token accounts, ordinary accounts, program IDs, fee payers, and blockhashes. These checks do not prove provider provenance, account ownership, semantic correctness, or trustless historical completeness; wallet-history completeness remains provider-attested.
- Controlled live validation remains separately authorized and excluded from deterministic regression. The first pre-hardening run, the distinct later post-hardening run, and the final post-remediation controlled live validation all passed their respective implementations. After patch 3, the final post-patch-3 controlled live validation **PASS**ed as the final authoritative release-gate run on Solana mainnet-beta with `lookback_7d_v1`. It examined two pages, terminated with `historical_bound_reached`, observed 76 canonical signatures, excluded zero post-anchor signatures, reconciled five in-window and five Enhanced transactions, and retained dispositions of 1 supported, 0 unsupported, 1 ambiguous, 3 unrelated, and 0 failed. Patch 3 changed no aggregate classification relative to the prior run; the newer finalized anchor naturally changed the content-addressed digests.
- v1.14.1 diagnostic observability is prospective only. It cannot localize an existing v1 controlled-live report, and the defensive `internal_boundary` / `none` / `unlocalized_malformed_response` tuple remains possible for an untrusted or newly introduced malformed path that lacks private fixed-enum provenance.
- Diagnostic stage, operation, and reason values identify a local rejection class, not provider intent, provider provenance, transaction truth, or historical completeness. No raw provider response is retained.
- Zero candidates was valid and not a validation failure; live candidate resolution and Slice 7 were not exercised, and no receipt or package digest was issued. The live release gate is complete, no further live rerun is required before tagging v1.14.0, and v1.14.0 is not yet tagged. Wallet-history completeness remains provider-attested, not trustless.
- A provider-attested clean candidate is not a complete-wallet performance statement, portfolio statement, track record, receipt, proof, or authorization token.
- Trading-fund custody or control is outside the system boundary.

These limits fail closed. They do not imply authorization for future hosted, live, storage, publication, or transaction-signing work.

The provider-neutral core has no global fetch, direct environment access, filesystem credential reads, package store, publication, signing, minting, or deployment. The controlled-live executable intentionally has only the separately sanctioned environment-key, global-fetch HTTP, monotonic timing, and one sanitized mode-0600 temporary-report capabilities. Deterministic regression runs its operator tests offline with injected ports and may create isolated temporary report fixtures.
