# Artifact v1.14 Limitations

Artifact v1.14 Wallet-Wide Bounded Acquisition v1 is deliberately conservative:

- Completeness is provider-attested, not a trustless cryptographic proof against malicious or jointly inconsistent providers.
- The current adapter supports Helius plus Solana RPC only.
- Acquisition supports Solana mainnet-beta only and pins its genesis hash.
- Only `lookback_7d_v1`, `lookback_30d_v1`, `lookback_90d_v1`, and `lookback_180d_v1` are understood.
- There is no arbitrary historical end date and pagination must begin at latest state.
- High-activity wallets may exhaust safe page, transaction, retry, or timeout budgets; the result fails rather than truncates.
- One source transaction may produce at most one supported event.
- Multi-operation, multi-swap, swap-plus-distinct-transfer, batch, and multiple-output transactions are unsupported or ambiguous rather than partially accepted.
- Fee-payer and wallet-ownership policy is conservative. Delegated or sponsored swaps are not supported events.
- v1.14 has no live marks. Open and realized-partial unrealized PnL therefore remains unavailable.
- Accounting remains raw quote only. There is no cross-quote or USD normalization.
- Retained Solana RPC envelopes do not currently exist; exact RPC transcript retention remains future evidence work.
- The exact retained Helius shape set is limited to `jup_buy`, `jup_sell`, `ray_buy`, `ray_sell`, and `jupiter_close_account_swap`.
- Finalized RPC pages are synthetic fixtures. Orca, UNKNOWN, failed, mixed-disposition, ambiguity, and provider-error bodies are also synthetic unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- There is no hosted persistence, authentication, worker system, product API, or product UI.
- There is no publication, package-store commit, upload, signing, minting, or deployment in this boundary.
- There is no privacy guarantee against correlation with public-chain wallet activity.
- Lexical and sensitive-value validation still depends partly on the trusted provider projection for identity-bearing values.
- Controlled live validation remains separately authorized and is excluded from deterministic regression. Live validation was not performed during v1.14 closeout.
- A provider-attested clean candidate is not a complete-wallet performance statement, portfolio statement, track record, receipt, proof, or authorization token.
- Trading-fund custody or control is outside the system boundary.

These limits fail closed. They do not imply authorization for future hosted, live, storage, publication, or transaction-signing work.
