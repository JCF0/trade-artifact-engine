## Why the complete correction needs another allocation contract

Reusing the **design** means discarding a failed round in full, not selecting a
convenient classic response from one attempt and Token-2022 from another. For the
current stricter predicate, a fresh round must reacquire together as one bounded
logical group:

1. Direct wallet + both ATA accounts (`getMultipleAccounts`).
2. Classic owner population.
3. Token-2022 owner population.

The direct request retains a nondecreasing floor; the two owner requests derive
their floor from that round's direct response. Only all-three-equal, internally
consistent evidence can survive. Repeating only the pair might coincidentally
return the old slot, but cannot recover the general advancing-slot case. No
standard-RPC exact-slot selector can force that old boundary.

The frozen `full-context-lag-v1/session-spec.json:35–53` and
`RUN-PLAN-successor.md:41–61` retain non-transferable logical maxima of:

- `getMultipleAccounts: 2` — direct opening plus required route construction.
- `getTokenAccountsByOwner: 2` — one complete pair.

One complete restart needs **3** multiple-account calls including the downstream
route, and **4** owner calls. Reusing the route allowance strands construction.
The existing two additional physical attempts apply only after explicit correlated
-32016 and repeat identical method/parameters. They are not permission for fresh
successful-response resampling, and one fresh triple costs three calls anyway.

Whole-sequence arithmetic from the actual frozen method map:

| Case | Nine history calls* | Fifteen history calls (maximum) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original full sequence | 23 | 29 |
| Plus one fresh triple | 26 | 32 |
| Plus fresh triple and two existing lag attempts | 28 | 34 |

*One nonempty scan page, empty exhaustion page and stable repeated head for each
of three addresses. This is the shortest successful history shape, not a promised

omitted merely to make room for simulation.

Spare total capacity on a short history does not override per-method ceilings.
Conversely, granting new method maxima alone does not make maximum-length history,
resampling and lag recovery all fit within 29 requests or the wall-clock deadline.

## Coherent successor decision, before any external proposal

The recommended future change is **acquisition policy only, not evidence semantics**:
explicitly authorize at most one complete three-read restart on valid watermark
mismatch, changing qualification logical maxima to multiple-accounts 3 / owner 4,
while retaining total 29 and all other ceilings. This recommendation is unimplemented
and is not an external-session authorization. If method maxima must remain exactly
unchanged, retain the current fail-closed worker and do not propose a retrying run.

A successor would need all of these together, not incremental provider diagnostics:

- Separate durable round reservation/completion and accepted-round identity;
  exact RPC ordinal/physical-attempt accounting; no refund, resume or sample mixing.
- Preserve the original genesis/anchor and absolute deadline. A later round cannot
  rewrite the age anchor or erase post-anchor history. Any anchor refresh would
  require reacquiring slot/block and redoing dependent history/age evidence under
  its own explicit allocation, not merely relabeling the existing anchor.
- Revalidate every round's direct data and both populations; changed/malformed
  accounts or cross-call conflicts remain terminal semantic stops, not reasons
  to keep sampling until an inconvenient observation disappears. Bind retained
  round evidence even when stopping before both lanes complete.
- Keep correlated -32016 recovery session-global (at most two, identical parameters,
  250/500 ms), distinct from valid-result watermark resampling. Retried responses
  still must satisfy that round's floor and equality. Never reset time or count.
- Preserve sequential transport unless concurrency itself is explicitly qualified;
  sequential whole-round resampling is possible but cannot guarantee equal slots.
  No transport/auth/other-error retries and no simulation retry.
- Reserve downstream capacity before admitting a restart; maximum-length histories
  plus recovery cannot all fit. Exhaustion must stop with retained partial evidence,
  never skip histories, setup samples, fee, expiry or the simulation objective.
- Derive all later history/route floors from the accepted complete round. Route
  pool/ticks/vaults, blockhash, exact message fee, expiry and one unsigned simulation
  stay fresh dependent observations, not reused failed-round/provider-session data.
- Retain 5 s/request, 50 s work / 55 s management / 60 s launch, 1 MiB/request and
  response, 16 MiB aggregate response, total 29. Backoff, retention and construction
  consume the same absolute work interval. Partial/uncertain reservations stay spent.

locally for advancing-slot recovery, persistent mismatch, owner/fee context lag
