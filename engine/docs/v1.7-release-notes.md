# v1.7 Release Notes

## Summary

Artifact v1.7 adds reusable receipt-scoped Coverage Statements across local proof and receipt-board surfaces.

Coverage Statements describe what a selected receipt itself covers. They do not describe a wallet, trader, profile, portfolio, full track record, skill, prize eligibility, or anti-wash-trading status.

## What v1.7 Adds

v1.7 adds:
- deterministic `receipt_coverage_v1` Coverage Statements
- receipt-board JSON coverage integration
- proof-detail and verifier JSON coverage exposure
- compact coverage display on proof-card and receipt-board previews
- compact coverage display on static proof export and hosted-preview pages
- v1.7 regression runner
- v1.7 coverage demo checker

## Coverage Boundary

Coverage remains receipt-scoped and raw-quote only:
- `scope_type`: `receipt`
- `coverage_basis`: `canonical_inventory_receipt`
- `valuation_status`: `raw_quote`
- `usd_normalized`: `false`

Coverage event bounds are receipt event bounds only. They do not prove complete wallet history, historical balances, portfolio history, trader skill, or complete market participation.

## Compatibility Boundary

v1.7 does not change:
- canonical v1.2 receipt schema or hashes
- accounting
- normalization
- ledger or candidate generation
- verifier result logic
- trust strength
- board eligibility or ranking
- manifest selection
- gallery tiles
- upload, mint, signing, deployment, accounts, or profiles

Closed verified receipts remain the only board-eligible type.

## Deferred

Same-mint multi-input swap aggregation remains deferred to a later slice. Gallery coverage display remains deferred.
