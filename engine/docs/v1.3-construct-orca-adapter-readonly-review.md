## B1 — Downward-rounded minimum output does not enforce a strict maximum of 50 bps

Location: `engine/src/verification-scope-v1-3/final-proof-agent/orca-message-boundary-v1.mjs:31–33`; serialized at `:61`.

The condition compares the supplied minimum to integer division of `quote * (10000 - maximum_slippage_bps) / 10000`. Division truncates downward. Therefore a minimum just below the exact permitted lower bound passes whenever that product is not divisible by 10000.

Concrete arithmetic, computed without importing or running repository code:

- Quoted output: `10000001` raw units.
- Mandated maximum: `50` bps.
- Exact lower bound: `1990000199/200` raw units, i.e. `9950000.995`.
- Current accepted integer minimum: `9950000`.
- Strict-bound cross-products: `9950000 * 10000 = 99500000000`, below `10000001 * 9950 = 99500009950`.
- At that accepted minimum, slippage is `500010000/10000001` bps, greater than 50.

Reachable scenario: executor-owned capture supplies that quote and rounded minimum, with the correct fee and a fee-message hash for the resulting message; readiness issues its correctly bound challenge; the authorized agent directs acquisition. The composition accepts the source commitment (`orca-signing-composition-v1.mjs:34–45`), construction accepts the rounded minimum, and that minimum is placed in the swap instruction. Admission/checkpoint/signature checks authenticate the resulting bytes but do not independently tighten this lower bound. No malicious Hermes input, forged signature, corrupted database, or administrator restore is necessary. This describes static reachability; no transaction was built or signed during review.

The excess is less than one raw output unit, but the current mandate and checklist claim a maximum of 50 bps without an explicit raw-unit rounding allowance. An operational quote adapter's future existence does not establish that allowance. This is an adapter constraint/contract issue, not the acknowledged absence of live capture.

Required resolution before an unqualified PASS: enforce the exact inequality (equivalently the integer ceiling of the lower bound), or obtain an explicit authoritative definition that permits downward raw-unit rounding and reconcile the claim/tests accordingly. This review does not authorize changing frozen economics or silently selecting such a tolerance.

### Required regression gap

`orca-message-boundary-v1.test.mjs:13–17,62–66` uses quoted outputs whose 50-bps lower bounds are integral. Its one-unit-below test (`:76–80`) consequently cannot expose B1. Add a non-divisible quote case with the exact cross-product oracle and its adjacent integer thresholds. Exercise the same case through authenticated composition with a correctly rebound readiness digest/fee-message hash, requiring rejection before signer invocation under the strict maximum contract. Do not merely mutate an already-issued source digest, since that tests source identity rather than the slippage gate.

## Assessment of the other requested boundaries

### 1. Exact message mapping

Apart from B1, mapping is direct and byte-bound. `orca-message-boundary-v1.mjs:24–67` validates the frozen mandate, phase/ordinal, input/full-disposal equality, fee equality, tick inputs, and canonical blockhash; constructs fixed account metas and one legacy Whirlpool instruction; and encodes amount, minimum, price limit and direction explicitly. The compiled fee payer is the required signer and writable fee payer even though its instruction meta requests no additional writability. No caller-provided extra instructions or account list enters construction.

`orca-signing-composition-v1.mjs:46–56` commits the entire private plan, including its message and construction-input digest. `:75–80` derives the signer message from that plan, copies callback input/output buffers, and checks returned wire against the retained expected message. Branding/byte equality rejects a caller-authored replacement plan; branding alone is not authorization.

### 2. Trusted wiring versus untrusted ingress

The factory accepts privileged capture, signer, and authority ports (`orca-signing-composition-v1.mjs:13–25`). Someone able to replace those ports or invoke their own raw signer is outside its trusted-wiring boundary. This is not protected against arbitrary same-process code and is not claimed to be.

Hermes ingress accepts only canonical authenticated decision bytes and time, not source/plan/state/signer objects. `wiggles-authenticated-control-plane-v1.mjs:155–172` verifies the signature and configured principal/static bindings before loading durable state/challenge. Source capture is snapshotted, closed-schema checked and challenge-bound. Its truth/freshness remains explicitly a trusted capture obligation, not something its self-hash proves.

### 3. Disposal authority

`orca-signing-composition-v1.mjs:28–43` reads current durable quantity and compares it and acquisition evidence against admission/challenge. `sqlite-decision-authority-v1.mjs:820–825` independently requires the acquisition ordinal to be FINALIZED and its evidence to match before disposal reservation. The existing source-bound acquisition closure path supplies the retained state, rather than accepting a Hermes-selected amount. The standalone builder's equal supplied quantities are not themselves authority, as the documentation correctly states.

### 4. Checkpoints before signing

`offline-executor-core-v1.mjs:95–138` performs authenticated admission, durable consumption, preparation and key-load ambiguity checkpoint before calling the private signer. The new wrapper additionally reconciles stage, admission, prepared digest and semantic digest and checks revocation (`orca-signing-composition-v1.mjs:61–77`). No callback invocation precedes those checks in the inspected path.

### 5. Verified wire and retention before release

`orca-signed-wire-boundary-v1.mjs:16–29` requires one signature, exact expected-message bytes, canonical legacy reserialization and Ed25519 verification against the message's first required signer. In composition that signer is fixed by the constructed mandate wallet, not chosen from arbitrary callback wire.

Retention uses exclusive ordinal-specific creation, private-root checks, file fsync, directory fsync, no-follow reopen, bounded metadata and byte readback (`:33–65`). `wiggles-authenticated-control-plane-v1.mjs:90–110` then requires successful durable signed-intent recording before returning the intent. SQLite independently rereads and verifies retained bytes and reconciles the prepared record (`sqlite-decision-authority-v1.mjs:421–470,942–985`). An exception or revoked result prevents release. Same-UID malicious filesystem replacement remains outside the approved deployment assumption.

### 6. Failure, concurrency and restart

The private plan is removed before awaiting inspection/signing. Durable reservation, not that process-local map, arbitrates multiple instances. Existing ordinal identity returns ALREADY_CONSUMED or a conflict (`sqlite-decision-authority-v1.mjs:790–847`); the core requires CONSUMED before preparation. Prepared and ambiguity transitions cannot repeat or move backward (`:919–940`). Reopen cannot reconstruct a fresh signing permission through this composition from a consumed ordinal. File creation also refuses overwrite. No automatic replacement path was identified under ordinary restart and trusted administration. An injected signer implementing its own retries would violate trusted signer wiring; the wrapper is not a sandbox for a malicious callback.

### 7. Test meaning and limits

The seven tests are meaningful component/composition tests, not a successful live-wallet end-to-end demonstration:

- Mapping assertions inspect actual decoded instruction data and selected accounts. The every-byte alteration loop proves equality checking, not independently that every originally constructed privilege/PDA is semantically correct.
- Positive crypto/retention uses a fixed test key and a zero-instruction test message. This proves cryptographic identity/retention utility behavior without asserting that such a message satisfies the Orca mandate.
- Final-proof-shaped tests exercise authenticated SQLite composition with inert callbacks, checkpoint observation, source/fee binding rejection and ordinary reopen. They do not cover successful production signing, and the docs explicitly acknowledge that distinction.

B1's non-integral rounding test is required for the claimed strict constraint. Additional useful assurance, not independently demonstrated slice defects: explicit assertions for the complete compiled privilege vector/oracle/tick-PDA order, and an ordinal-2 composed-path test reaching the inert callback from genuine retained acquisition closure. Existing generic authority concurrency/crash tests should not be relabeled as new adapter-specific fault-injection tests. No real wallet key or weakened production mandate is required or authorized by this review.

### 8. Live entry point

`live-executor-composition-v1.mjs:9–14` accepts no fields and unconditionally throws `bounded_agent_live_executor_not_released`; it imports no signer and does not route into the new composition. The intended live entry point remains disabled.
