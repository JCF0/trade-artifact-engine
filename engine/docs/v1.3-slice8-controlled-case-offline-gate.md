## Offline-to-mainnet mapping

### Semantic evidence requirements already enforced by Artifact

- finalized wallet-wide transaction acquisition and authoritative transaction transcript;
- source-bound transaction-effect projection;
- independently complete/all-or-error classic and Token-2022 owner-enumeration lanes;
- concurrent whole-pair capture, whole-pair retry, and equal finalized-watermark `context.slot` consistency;
- `minContextSlot` represented only as a freshness lower bound;
- explicit `atomic_snapshot = false` evidence and rejection of standard-RPC equal-watermark pairs as combined `COMPLETE` / `EXACT_*` boundary authority;
- separately admitted `ARTIFACT_CONTROLLED_CROSS_CALL_QUIESCENCE_ASSUMPTION_V1` evidence for this controlled experiment;
- exhaustive Position economic-effect classification through the registered capability;
- exact Position Episodes, non-interference, exhaustive population, explicit selection, and immutable claim validation.

For a classic-SPL target with an explicitly empty Token-2022 target population, no Token-2022 mint-account lookup is required. The owner-enumeration/mint-read atomicity limitation is therefore irrelevant to this first classic-SPL proof. The two owner enumerations remain non-atomic: equal slots mean the same finalized commitment watermark, not one indexed snapshot.

### Operational sequencing requirements for the controlled experiment

The experiment must document and supervise: wallet preparation; opening finalized capture; acquisition signing; finality confirmation; full-disposal signing; ending finalized capture; and secure termination of signing access. Signing and submission under Artifact's control must be disabled throughout each opening/ending capture interval. Before the ending capture, all controlled submissions must stop and all controlled in-flight transactions must be drained and finalized. Both provider reads are concurrent and must report an equal finalized watermark.

Standard RPC cannot cryptographically exclude unsolicited third-party account creation or transfers during either cross-call interval. `ARTIFACT_CONTROLLED_CROSS_CALL_QUIESCENCE_ASSUMPTION_V1` admits that non-interference only as an explicit controlled-experiment assumption. It is not atomic, cryptographic, trustless, or provider-proven quiescence. Slice 8 does not attest signer release, revocation, custody, or key destruction.

A manual/supervised protocol is sufficient for the first controlled experiment if it is documented, observed, and makes no signer-custody, automated-release, revocation, or key-destruction claim. A code-enforced signer supervisor is not semantically necessary for this narrowly described experiment. It becomes necessary before Artifact claims or relies on machine-enforced custody/revocation guarantees, unattended operation, or repeatable automated boundary sequencing.
