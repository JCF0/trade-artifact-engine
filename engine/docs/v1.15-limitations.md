# Artifact v1.15 Limitations

- Wallet-history completeness remains provider-attested. Finalized `getSignaturesForAddress` is the sole completeness authority; neither bulk full history nor fallback proves trustless completeness.
- Production is pinned to Helius at `https://mainnet.helius-rpc.com/` and Helius-exclusive `getTransactionsForAddress`. Multiple providers and caller-selected origins are unsupported.
- Bulk enrichment is pinned to `transactionDetails: "full"`, `commitment: "finalized"`, `encoding: "json"`, `maxSupportedTransactionVersion: 0`, `tokenAccounts: "none"`, `status: "any"`, `sortOrder: "desc"`, and page size: 100 with opaque pagination semantics.
- Scope is the direct wallet only. Associated-token-account expansion is deferred.
- Finalized `getTransaction` is missing-signature-only fallback, explicitly budgeted from zero through eight. It cannot repair contradictory or malformed bulk evidence.
- Provider duplicates, omissions, pagination behavior, schema compatibility, lifecycle wording, metering, and documented coverage limitations remain provider assumptions. Drift or uncertainty means STOP.
- Full transaction versions beyond Solana v0, null block times, absent metadata, unsupported instruction families, unresolved wallet effects, complex batches/multi-swaps, sponsored fee payers, and unknown ownership fail closed or remain unsupported/ambiguous.
- Latest-state whole-wallet lookback windows are mutable and are not frozen golden regression vectors. New unrelated activity can enter the window, older activity can leave it, and a newly observed wallet-wide unresolved effect can stop issuance even when a retained target trade remains exactly reconstructable.
- `unknown_token_scope`, including unknown token ownership or unbound mint scope, remains wallet-wide uncertainty. Artifact does not infer that missing ownership evidence means no effect.
- Unmatched wallet-affecting instructions and unsupported nested program shapes remain unresolved unless their effects are explicitly decoded and reconciled. A recognized outer router does not make an arbitrary inner program harmless.
- Broad program/instruction decoding, support for new DEX/program shapes, and complex batch or multi-swap reconstruction are deferred.
- Associated-token-account expansion, arbitrary historical end dates, target-only history authority, multiple providers, and transaction versions beyond Solana v0 are deferred.
- Wallet-wide fail-closed semantics are unchanged. A semantic SAFE_FAILURE proves conservative refusal, not successful reconstruction of unsupported transactions and not absence of economic effect.
- The five dispositions and all authoritative v1 acquisition, candidate, Slice 7, receipt, and package schemas remain unchanged.
- Legacy Enhanced is currently documented as a legacy product in maintenance mode. v1.15 production does not depend on it; retained Enhanced code/data is offline identity-oracle infrastructure only.
- Controlled-live reports are sanitized operational evidence, not authoritative artifacts. They use `artifact_v1.15_controlled_live_validation_v1` and never retain raw responses, URLs, credentials, headers, tokens, prose, stacks, or filesystem provenance.
- Report privacy does not prevent correlation with public-chain wallet activity.
- No package-store production write, publication, upload, signing, minting, deployment, hosted persistence, product API, or product UI is in this slice.
- The stable positive modernization evidence is the reviewed Slice 7 individual-versus-bulk full-transaction capture plus deterministic v2 replay of the exact retained JUP/RAY transactions. Controlled-live reports remain sanitized operational evidence rather than retained raw fixtures.
- The final JUP whole-wallet control ended in an accepted downstream semantic refusal. It issued no authoritative acquisition result or candidate set, did not reach Slice 7, and provided no live receipt/package identity comparison.
- Provider, authentication, schema, completeness, pagination, duplicate-conflict, reconciliation, source-mismatch, cap, deadline, timeout, fallback, capability, report, and privacy failures remain release-blocking and are not covered by the semantic-refusal exception.
- Artifact v1.15 is release-ready with these nonblocking limitations but remains uncommitted and untagged until separately authorized Git/tag actions occur.

The system deterministically reconstructs supported results from provider-attested on-chain evidence and exposes the evidence boundaries and limitations required to reproduce its result.
